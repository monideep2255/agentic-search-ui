## Decision cadence

Product-owner rule, 2026-09-27: ask a decision that is the owner's the moment it comes up. Never hold it for a daily list, a status report or a batch.

1. Ask at once through the question tool: yes, no or pick-one, the recommendation first, each option's cost in plain words. Send a phone notification at the same moment, one line under 200 characters.
2. Keep working on everything that does not wait on the answer. Dependent work stops at a clean point.
3. Before the owner sleeps, goes busy or starts an overnight run, ask every foreseeable decision.
4. While the owner is away, take the recommended option only where a standing owner decision covers it. Otherwise queue the question, notify, and ask on their return.
5. One question per decision. Several independent decisions may share one question-tool call, each its own question.
6. Every decision and its answer becomes a `DECISIONS.md` row.

Applies wherever a rule or skill escalates to the owner: bossman mode, the UI fix loop, `/verify`, `/standup`, `/phase-checkpoint`. The owner's words, the reasons and the permissions: `.claude/rules-reference/decision-cadence.md`.

The test: did this decision reach the owner the moment it came up, as its own question with a notification, or did it wait for a list?
