## What we covered

- Phase 5 opening
  - Scoped against the locked PRD, technical specification, and evaluation playbook, all three frozen until Step 6.2.
  - Product owner challenged the premise of adding skills at all: weave in what exists first, then build only what is missing.
  - Reframed Step 5.2 from "which skills do we build" to a coverage question.
- Method
  - Product owner directed use of `plan-then-fan-out` and `parallel-first`.
  - Ten Sonnet workers in parallel on non-overlapping slices, reasoning model kept planning and synthesis.
  - Demand workers were denied access to the rule files so they could not talk themselves into false matches. Every proposed match verified during synthesis.
  - 303 obligations extracted, 191 owned, 57 unowned.
- Step 5.1, the build harness
  - Found a live defect: bossman-mode and release-workflow created `feature/description` branches against the `phase/N.M-description` convention, which also silently disabled ship's MR step.
  - Rewired to tech spec Section 25's 26 build phases, worktree isolation by default, product owner role, scope check, Playwright gate, six-skill phase-end chain.
- Step 5.2, skills and rules
  - Two new skills: `task-tracker` and `learnings`.
  - `eval-harness` rewritten; it had never referenced the evaluation playbook and was missing 13 of its 17 demands.
  - Three rules added or adopted, two extended, one narrowed.
  - Zero of the four skills Plan.md originally floated were built.
- Step 5.3, root documents
  - 19 stale statements corrected, PR template rewritten off inherited BioLink and KGX gates, three non-resolving slash commands fixed.
- Step 5.4, reference documentation
  - `docs/Tool_implementation_mechanics.md`, 19 per-tool API traps.
- The software team operating model
  - Product owner described the target: lead engineer plus team, Linear or Jira style tracking with acceptance criteria, phase-wise review, learnings capture, branches and worktrees, PR review then merge.
  - Product owner is called at inspection points and phase end, coordinates only with the lead engineer, who holds full authority over execution method.
  - Product owner asked whether stacking merge requests delays development. It does not; the build continues on the next branch, only the merge waits. Merging overnight buys no throughput for dependent phases at all, so autonomy would only remove the review gate.
- Decisions taken
  - `dependency-tracking` narrowed to hooks only, since the field was absent from 9 of 13 skills and wrong where present.
  - Overnight unattended execution deferred. The standing deny on proceeding without approval holds.
  - Playwright becomes the UI verification gate, wired in Phase 6 after supply-chain checks.
  - `.claude/` and `.codex` re-tracked in git for the duration of v1, reversing the previous day's untracking, with `settings.local.json` staying ignored.
