# Retest, product owner

Every change that is built, live on develop and waiting for the product owner to try it. This file is the one place retests are listed: a card moves here the day it goes live, and leaves when the product owner gives a verdict.

- Each row names the queries to type, by number, in `testing/Test_queries_and_workflows.md`, where each query says what you should see.
- Your verdict closes a row: Approved removes it here and sets its status in `testing/UI_fixes_done.md`, "Done features at a glance"; defects send the card back to the board's To do in `testing/UI_fix_plan.md` with your words.
- A wording or layout card the product reviewer passed at both widths closes by itself seven days after it lands here unless you object (`DECISIONS.md`, 2026-09-25).
- Moved here from `testing/UI_fixes_done.md` on 2026-10-15 at the product owner's request, so every retest lives in one file of its own.

Last updated: 2026-10-16.
