DISEASE ASSOCIATIONS AND GENE FUNCTION

BRCA1 encodes a nuclear phosphoprotein that maintains genomic stability and acts as a tumor suppressor.⁠23 Its protein product participates in transcription, double-strand break repair, and recombination.⁠23 Mutations in BRCA1 account for approximately 40% of inherited breast cancers and more than 80% of inherited breast and ovarian cancers.⁠23
