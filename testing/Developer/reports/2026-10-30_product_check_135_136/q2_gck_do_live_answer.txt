The enzyme operates in a tissue-specific manner, playing a role in glucose-stimulated insulin secretion in the pancreas and in glucose uptake and conversion to glycogen in the liver.⁠20 Found 1 gene record for GCK: glucokinase.⁠1

The gene summary notes that, unlike other hexokinases, this enzyme is not inhibited by its product glucose-6-phosphate and remains active while glucose is abundant.⁠20 Multiple promoters and alternative splicing produce distinct protein isoforms with tissue-specific expression.⁠20

DISEASE ASSOCIATIONS

Mutations in GCK that alter enzyme activity have been associated with multiple types of diabetes and hyperinsulinemic hypoglycemia.⁠20
