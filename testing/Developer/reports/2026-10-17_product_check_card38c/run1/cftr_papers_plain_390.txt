A paper on gene regulation examines a control region upstream of the CFTR gene, and another explores how splicing factors cause a part of the CFTR genetic instructions to be skipped.⁠4, 8 I found 54 published papers related to CFTR, out of 1962 available.⁠1–20

One identifies a patient with a D579G mutation who has milder lung involvement and sufficient pancreatic function, another reports a D513G mutation found in a French patient with a condition involving the vas deferens.⁠2, 6

Another studies how a separate gene affects liver disease severity in cystic fibrosis.⁠29
