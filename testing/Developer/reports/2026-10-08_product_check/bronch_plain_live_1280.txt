Bronchiolitis is the most common lower respiratory tract infection to affect infants and toddlers.⁠1

In healthy infants and children, bronchiolitis goes away on its own.⁠1 Treatment is usually aimed at relieving symptoms, and the main goal is to keep the child properly oxygenated and hydrated.⁠1
