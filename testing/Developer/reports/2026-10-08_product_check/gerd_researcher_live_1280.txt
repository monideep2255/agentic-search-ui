GERD is a gastrointestinal motility disorder arising from reflux of stomach contents into the esophagus or oral cavity, producing symptoms or complications.⁠1

RISK FACTORS AND PATHOGENESIS

Factors contributing to GERD pathogenesis include abnormal transient relaxations of the lower esophageal sphincter, defects in esophageal mucosal defense, impaired esophageal and gastric motility and clearance, and anatomical defects of the lower esophageal reflux barrier such as hiatal hernia.⁠2 GERD may also lead to erosive esophagitis, esophageal strictures, and Barrett esophagus, a precursor to esophageal adenocarcinoma.⁠1
