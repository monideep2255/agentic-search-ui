The typical symptoms of GERD are heartburn and regurgitation of stomach contents into the throat.⁠1 GERD is a condition where stomach contents flow back into the esophagus or mouth, causing symptoms or complications.⁠1 It can affect quality of life and may lead to more serious problems including inflammation of the esophagus, narrowing of the esophagus, and Barrett esophagus, which can be a precursor to esophageal cancer.⁠1 Factors that can contribute to GERD include problems with the esophageal lining's defenses, impaired movement and clearing of the esophagus and stomach, and anatomical defects such as hiatal hernia.⁠2
