## How it was checked

| Check | Method |
|-------|--------|

| (d) Status | The files compared with `HANDOFF.md`, `PROGRESS.md`, `requirements/Plan.md`'s status table, the merge history, `gh release list` and both live `/health` endpoints |
| (e) Stack and architecture | Spot checks against `requirements.txt`, `pyproject.toml`, `frontend/package.json`, `harness/tiers.py`, `core/graph.py`, the tool modules, the MCP server, `graph_connection.py` and `graph_http_transport.py` |
| (f) Style and privacy | A grep for em and en dashes, bold, vendor names, local paths and server addresses, then `check_style.py` on every changed file, `check_doc_drift.py --check`, `check_living_docs.py` and `tests/tracker/test_check_doc_drift.py` |

Only `CLAUDE.md` was edited by hand. `AGENTS.md` was then regenerated with `CLAUDE_PROJECT_DIR=<worktree> bash .claude/hooks/sync-agents-md.sh --force`, and its body is identical to `CLAUDE.md` from line 4 onward.
