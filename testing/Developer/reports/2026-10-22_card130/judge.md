## Findings

### J-130-01: the column findings swap the completeness repair's directive from "the records you left out" to "the organisms"
- Severity: major (behaviour change the PR does not state; unsure whether it is worse for the reader)
- What: `core/graph.py:14451` starts the parallel draft whenever `_listing_uncitable` (`core/graph.py:10473-10489`) is non-empty. That function compares prompt findings against the listing's cited ids and has no `row_detail` exclusion, and the listing never cites a column finding, so every list with columns starts the draft with `build_listing_gap_directive` (`synthesis/findings.py:1945`) naming the columns. When the repair is needed, `core/graph.py:14674-14675` takes that already running draft instead of the sequential `build_completeness_directive(omitted_findings)` call at `core/graph.py:14676-14690`. So the repair no longer names the omitted records.
- Reproduction: my probe (scratchpad `probes/e2e.py`, 40 real-shaped ortholog rows through `cypher_query` with a faked executor, `write_node` with a faked writer that grounds nothing, Researcher). Develop base 68586a65: second writer call contains `COMPLETENESS CORRECTION. Your previous answer omitted findings` and a block `[1] name=tumor protein p53` to `[30] name=tumor protein p53` (60 numbered lines). Branch: second call contains `COMPLETENESS REQUIREMENT. The system lists the records found below your answer` and a block of only `[2] organism=Mus musculus`, `[4] organism=Rattus norvegicus`, up to `[30] organism=Oryctolagus cuniculus` (45 numbered lines).
- Why it matters: the measured ortholog write speedup (median 9.4 s to 2.5 s, after.md) most likely comes from this swap (a parallel draft in place of a sequential repair), not from the writer seeing columns. It also changes what the repair asks for in the 17 of 24 fallback runs. The PR body says the draft "costs money, not time" and is "kept only when the existing repair rule needs it". The keep rule is unchanged, but the repair's prompt is not. The new test file's docstring (`tests/.../test_row_column_findings.py`, "so it cannot start a second writing call") is false: column findings start a second writing call on every list with columns.
- NOT FIXED

### J-130-02: the slot cost falls on other calls' findings too, so a short list with context loses context from the writer's prompt
- Severity: major
- What: the column finding takes no DISPLAY slot but does take a PROMPT slot. `prompt_findings = synth_findings[:_MAX_FINDINGS_FOR_MODEL_PROMPT]` (`core/graph.py:14278`, cap 30 at `core/graph.py:9305`) is sliced after the lead rows are numbered first (`synthesis/findings.py:754`), so every column finding pushes one context finding (a gene's live record, its summary, a paper) out of the 30. The PR and after.md describe the cost only as "15 records instead of 30" of the same list and say "A short list such as rs334 and rs6025 loses nothing".
- Reproduction: probe `orthologs12_ctx` (12 real-shaped ortholog rows as the lead call, one Layer 2 `gene` record with a 150 character summary and 12 `Publication` titles as context calls), echo writer, Researcher. Develop base: the first writer prompt numbers 26 findings, the last being `26 Publication title: Evolution of p53 family member study number 11 across vertebrates`. Branch: the prompt numbers 30 findings, 24 of them the 12 orthologs and their organisms, and stops at `30 Publication title: Evolution of p53 family member study number 3 across vertebrates`. Papers 4 to 11 never reach the writer. The measured set has no such shape: each column question's lead list was long enough to fill the prompt on both sides.
- Why it matters: on any question whose list has between about 8 and 30 rows plus context records, the writer loses those context records. A paper abstract or gene summary is the kind of finding the listing cannot cite (`build_listing_gap_directive` docstring), so the prose is the only place a reader sees it. That is a loss compared with develop on an unmeasured shape, which the acceptance sentence ("if nothing is worse than develop") does not allow.
- NOT FIXED
