BRCA1 is associated with familial cancer of breast, familial breast-ovarian cancer susceptibility 1, pancreatic cancer susceptibility 4, and Fanconi anemia complementation group S.⁠1–4

BRCA1-deficient cancer cells show heightened sensitivity to PARP inhibitors when ferroptosis protection is disrupted.⁠5

Preneoplastic stromal cells have been shown to promote BRCA1-mediated breast tumorigenesis.⁠6
