Research on Ashkenazi Jewish people has focused on three inherited cancer-risk gene changes—185delAG and 5382insC in BRCA1, and 6174delT in BRCA2—that together appear in roughly 2% of this population.⁠1 In a group of 92 Ashkenazi Jewish ovarian cancer patients, one in four carried one of these three founder changes.⁠2

DNA-based screening for Tay-Sachs appeared to produce fewer false alarms than the older enzyme method.⁠3

People who were married or living with a partner were about four times more likely to actually take the test.⁠4
