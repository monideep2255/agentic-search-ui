Does coffee help make exercise more effective?

Show work ▾

I found 5 published papers on this topic.⁠4–8

Caffeine appears to help both trained and untrained individuals.⁠1 That same review also reports that drinking caffeine as coffee appears less effective than taking pure caffeine.⁠2 Regarding cardiovascular health, a review found that moderate coffee consumption is associated with a reduced risk of hypertension, type 2 diabetes, heart attack, arrhythmias, and heart failure, though unfiltered coffee may raise LDL cholesterol and some effects may be independent of caffeine.⁠3
