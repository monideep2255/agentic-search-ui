I found 5 published papers on this topic.⁠3–7

Metformin lowers blood sugar mainly by reducing the liver's production of glucose, though scientists still debate exactly how it does this.⁠1 One well-known idea is that metformin activates an energy-sensing protein in cells called AMPK, but this may not explain everything the drug does.⁠2 There is also interest in metformin's possible roles in cardiovascular disease and cancer, though the mechanisms behind these effects remain unclear.⁠2
