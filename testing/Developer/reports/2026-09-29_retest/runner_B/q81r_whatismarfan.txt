Found 1 disease record for Marfan syndrome: Marfan syndrome.⁠3

Marfan syndrome (MFS) is a multisystem connective tissue disease with autosomal dominant inheritance, mainly caused by FBN1 gene mutation, with diverse clinical manifestations.⁠1 The disease has no radical treatment method, but early identification, diagnosis, and treatment can effectively prolong patient life span.⁠1

The Ghent II Nosology criteria are crucial in Marfan syndrome diagnosis, guiding clinicians in identifying high-risk patients.⁠2
