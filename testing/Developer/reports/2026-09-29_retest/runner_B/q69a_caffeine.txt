I found 5 published papers on this topic.⁠3–7

Aerobic endurance is the form of exercise that most consistently shows moderate-to-large benefits, though the size of the effect varies from person to person.⁠1

The popular theory that caffeine enhances fat burning and spares stored muscle energy has very little support.⁠2 Drinking caffeine as coffee appears less effective than taking pure caffeine.⁠2
