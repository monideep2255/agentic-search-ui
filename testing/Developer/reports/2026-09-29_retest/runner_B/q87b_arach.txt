BEALS-HECHT SYNDROME

Beals-Hecht syndrome, also known as congenital contractural arachnodactyly, is caused by a fibrillin defect shared with Marfan syndrome and is characterized by arachnodactyly alongside narrow body habitus, scoliosis, congenital contractures, and external ear deformities.⁠1
