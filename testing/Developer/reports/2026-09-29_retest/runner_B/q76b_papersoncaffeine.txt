I found 5 published papers on this topic.⁠4–8

Physical performance benefits, such as better endurance and muscle strength, appear at doses above about 200 milligrams.⁠1

When it comes to appetite and food intake, the evidence is mixed: coffee consumed a few hours before a meal had little effect on how much food people ate, while caffeine taken closer to mealtime may reduce short-term energy intake.⁠2 Evidence on how caffeine affects stomach emptying, appetite hormones, and feelings of hunger remains unclear.⁠2

Adults consume caffeine, and its use is increasing worldwide, with motivations including concentration, memory enhancement, and physical performance improvement.⁠1, 3
