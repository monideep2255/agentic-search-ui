I found 5 clinical trials related to melanoma.⁠4–8

Melanoma is a type of cancer that begins in pigment-producing cells called melanocytes and carries a high risk of spreading to other parts of the body.⁠1

Most of these subtypes share disruptions in the same cellular growth-control pathways, though the specific mechanism differs from one subtype to another.⁠2 Anorectal melanoma is one such aggressive mucosal subtype with a poor prognosis, and its best treatment approach is still being worked out.⁠3
