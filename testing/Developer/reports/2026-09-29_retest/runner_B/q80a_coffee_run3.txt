Notably, caffeine delivered as coffee appears to be ineffective compared with pure caffeine.⁠1

Caffeine consumption reduces total sleep time by 45 minutes and sleep efficiency by 7%, with recommendations that coffee be consumed at least 8.8 hours before bedtime to avoid sleep disruption.⁠2
