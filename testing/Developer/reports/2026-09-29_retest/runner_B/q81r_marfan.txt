CLINICAL FEATURES

MedGen lists these clinical features: Aortic regurgitation, Arachnodactyly, Astigmatism, Ectopia lentis, Esotropia, Exotropia, Pes planus, Glaucoma, Congestive heart failure and Hypertropia.⁠1–10 Early identification, diagnosis, and treatment can effectively prolong the life span of patients.⁠11
