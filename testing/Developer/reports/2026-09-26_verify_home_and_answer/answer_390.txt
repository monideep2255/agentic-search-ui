I found 1 gene related to BRCA1.⁠5

BRCA1 is a gene that helps keep DNA stable and stops tumors from forming.⁠1 The protein it makes joins with other proteins that sense DNA damage and send signals, forming a larger complex called the BRCA1-associated genome surveillance complex.⁠1 This protein also helps control how genes are read and helps fix serious DNA breaks.⁠1 When this gene is changed, it causes about 40% of breast cancers that run in families and over 80% of inherited breast and ovarian cancers combined.⁠1 Research shows BRCA1 works with a partner protein to guide DNA repair machinery to the right place, and also helps protect DNA ends during the repair process.⁠2, 3 Beyond DNA repair, BRCA1 also affects hormone and energy pathways in the body.⁠4
