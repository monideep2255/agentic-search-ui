I found 1 gene related to BRCA1.⁠4

BRCA1 is a gene that helps keep genetic material stable and acts as a tumor suppressor.⁠1 The protein it produces joins with other tumor-suppressing proteins and DNA-damage sensors to form a large complex that watches over the genome.⁠1 This protein is involved in reading genetic instructions, fixing serious DNA damage, and guiding genetic recombination.⁠1 When this gene is altered, it accounts for roughly 40% of inherited breast cancers and over 80% of inherited breast and ovarian cancers combined.⁠1 Research shows that BRCA1 works with a partner protein called BARD1 to help repair DNA by guiding a key pairing step and by protecting DNA ends during the repair process.⁠2, 3
