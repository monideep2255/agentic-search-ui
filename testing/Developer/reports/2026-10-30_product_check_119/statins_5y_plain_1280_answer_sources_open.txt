A review of statin intolerance found that muscle pain is the most common adverse effect and the most common reason people stop taking statins, and identified risk factors including unexplained muscle pain, a history of elevated creatine kinase levels, and a family history of muscle symptoms.⁠11 That review also noted that vitamin D and coenzyme Q supplementation may help with muscle-related effects, and that trying different statins or dosing schedules can improve tolerability.⁠11
