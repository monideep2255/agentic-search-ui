DISEASE OVERVIEW AND CFTR MODULATORS

Cystic fibrosis is described as a genetic and multisystemic disease whose main adult comorbidity is respiratory involvement with bronchiectasis, chronic bronchial infection, and airflow obstruction.⁠11 The treatment paradigm has shifted with the advent of CFTR modulators, which improve production of defective CFTR protein and increase its cell-surface expression, achieving better ion exchange, fluidising respiratory secretions, and reducing airflow obstruction.⁠11
