### A-15-04: The several-genes list reports "capped, total unknown" when every gene's full set was returned
- Severity: minor
- What: `hit_cap` turns on when `len(rows) >= branch_row_cap`, counting rows over all branches against ONE branch's share. With two genes (share 50), gene A with 30 variants and gene B with 25 gives 55 rows, no branch capped, and the result is reported `truncated=True, total_available=None` ("more than shown, total unknown"). The comment admits it "can claim less than is true". It never hides a gene: every branch carries its own scoping `LIMIT share` and the validator's per-branch `LIMIT 100`, and `genes * (100 // genes) <= 100` keeps the union within `execute_cypher`'s 100-row truncation, so the "per-gene share silently hides one gene" case was checked and does not occur.
- Reproduction: read from `_run_pipeline` and the template, not run against data with those counts. In the live run in A-15-03, 17 rows stayed under the share of 50, so the flag was correctly off.
- Why it matters: the person is told the list may be incomplete when it is complete. It is honest in direction but wrong in fact, and a per-branch row count (for example, any branch returning exactly `share`) would state it correctly.
- NOT FIXED
