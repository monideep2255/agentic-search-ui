I found 4 conditions related to CFTR.⁠1–4

CFTR is associated with four diseases: cystic fibrosis, hereditary pancreatitis, congenital bilateral aplasia of vas deferens from CFTR mutation, and bronchiectasis with or without elevated sweat chloride 1.⁠1–4

The most common mutation, called DeltaF508, prevents the protein from folding and being transported correctly within cells.⁠5
