### V-84S-02: The docstring's list of what produces the line leaves out one busy task, and its "up to 0.05 s short of the block" reads as if the figure were a lower bound on a block
- Severity: minor (documentation; inside this phase's fix round, which rewrote the docstring for A-84S-02 and J-84S-04)
- What: the docstring names three producers: "a frozen loop (one call that blocks), a loop kept busy by several tasks that each hold it in short chunks, and a host that pauses the whole process". One single task that runs 0.1 s chunks and yields with `await asyncio.sleep(0)` also writes lines, each about 0.26 s, though no single block exceeds 0.1 s. The paragraph "How far to trust the figure" says the figure "can be up to 0.05 s short of the block"; with several busy tasks the figure is many times longer than any block.
- Reproduction: my probe (`start_loop_stall_watch`, own handler on the module logger, branch code) for 2 s of one task looping `time.sleep(0.1); await asyncio.sleep(0)`: six lines, `event loop lag: 0.28 s`, `0.26 s`, `0.26 s`, `0.26 s`, `0.26 s`, `0.26 s`. Five such tasks, 10 chunks each: `1.55 s`, `1.51 s`, `1.51 s`, `0.47 s`; the longest single block was 0.1 s. Idle 3 s: no line. Single 0.4 s block: `0.37 s`. Single 2.0 s block: `1.97 s`.
- Why it matters: the closing sentence, "Read the count as 'the loop could not keep its timers', never as freezes only", is honest and covers this in principle, so the risk is small. But a reader of a week of 0.26 s lines, checking the three named causes, could rule out "one question doing CPU work in short pieces" and conclude a single call blocked for about 0.3 s. The fix is one more clause; no code change.
- NOT FIXED
