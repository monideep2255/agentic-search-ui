I found 5 published papers, 1 condition, 1 literature index entry and 5 clinical trials related to GERD.⁠3–14

GERD is a condition where stomach contents flow back into the esophagus or mouth, causing symptoms like heartburn and regurgitation.⁠1 It can hurt your quality of life and may lead to serious problems, including inflammation of the esophagus, narrowing of the esophagus, and a condition called Barrett esophagus that can precede esophageal cancer.⁠1

Symptoms in young children can be varied and unclear, so careful evaluation is needed.⁠2
