I found 5 published papers, 5 clinical trials, 1 condition and 1 literature index entry related to GERD.⁠3–14

The common symptoms of GERD are heartburn and regurgitation of stomach contents into the throat.⁠1 GERD happens when stomach contents flow back into the esophagus or mouth, causing symptoms or complications.⁠1 In children, GERD symptoms are varied and nonspecific, making diagnosis harder.⁠2 A natural history study is following GER in children and adolescents.⁠3
