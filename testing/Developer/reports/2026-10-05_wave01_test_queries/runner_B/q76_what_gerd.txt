I found 5 published papers, 1 condition, 1 literature index entry and 5 clinical trials related to GERD.⁠2–13

In children, ordinary reflux is common and usually normal, while GERD is a less common problem that causes troublesome symptoms and may need treatment.⁠1 Symptoms of GERD in young children can be varied and nonspecific, requiring careful evaluation.⁠1 It can involve factors like a weak lower esophageal sphincter, problems with esophageal defense, and anatomical issues such as hiatal hernia.⁠1
