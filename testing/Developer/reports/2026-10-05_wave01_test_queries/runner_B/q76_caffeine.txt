Physical performance benefits, such as improved time-to-exhaustion, muscle strength, and endurance, are evident after doses exceeding about 200 milligrams.⁠1

Evidence on whether coffee and caffeine influence food intake and appetite is unclear.⁠2 The evidence on whether caffeine and coffee affect stomach emptying, appetite hormones, and feelings of hunger is equivocal, and factors like a person's genetics for caffeine metabolism and sensitivity to bitter taste remain unknown.⁠2
