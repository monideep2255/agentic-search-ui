I found 4 published papers related to BRCA1.⁠1–4

BRCA1 is a gene that acts as a tumor suppressor and helps keep the genome stable.⁠19 Its protein helps repair DNA damage and is involved in transcription and recombination.⁠19 Changes in this gene account for about 40% of inherited breast cancers and more than 80% of inherited breast and ovarian cancers.⁠19
