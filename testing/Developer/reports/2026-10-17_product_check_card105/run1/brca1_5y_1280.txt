I found 4 published papers related to BRCA1.⁠1–4

The BRCA1 gene is a tumor suppressor that helps maintain genomic stability.⁠19 Its protein participates in transcription, double-strand break repair, and recombination.⁠19 Mutations in this gene account for roughly 40% of inherited breast cancers and over 80% of inherited breast and ovarian cancers.⁠19

From the paper "EXO1 protects BRCA1-deficient cells against toxic DNA lesions": BRCA1-mutated tumors show higher EXO1 expression and more genomic scarring compared with BRCA1-proficient tumors.⁠20 These findings point to EXO1 as a promising therapeutic target for BRCA1-deficient tumors.⁠20

Current preventive measures and targeted treatments for BRCA1/2 mutation carriers build on the concept of synthetic lethality.⁠21

BRCA1 is also linked to endocrine and metabolic regulation beyond cancer.⁠22 Interactions exist between BRCA1 and the insulin/IGF1 signaling pathway.⁠22 The review also discusses connections between metabolic disorders such as diabetes and BRCA1 mutations.⁠22
