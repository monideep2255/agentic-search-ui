I found 2 genes and 5 conditions related to MLH1, MSH2 and colorectal cancer.⁠1–7

MLH1 partners with another protein to form a complex that helps correct DNA mismatches, and it was identified as a gene frequently mutated in hereditary nonpolyposis colon cancer.⁠25 Research has used functional assays to test MLH1 and MSH2 variants together to identify patients with Lynch syndrome.⁠13

Published studies have also examined how MLH1 variants affect function using yeast and lab-based mismatch repair tests.⁠9 One trial focuses on identifying men with a genetic predisposition to prostate cancer.⁠12 Recent research also links MLH1 to breast cancer recurrence and to new gene-editing tools.⁠19, 16
