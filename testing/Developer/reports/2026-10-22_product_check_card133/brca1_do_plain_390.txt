BRCA1 is a gene that helps keep our DNA stable and acts as a tumor suppressor, meaning it helps stop cells from turning cancerous.⁠19 I found 1 gene related to BRCA1.⁠1

The protein it makes teams up with other protective proteins, DNA damage sensors, and signaling molecules to form a large complex that watches over the genome.⁠19 This protein is involved in transcription, fixing double-strand breaks in DNA, and genetic recombination.⁠19

The gene itself spans 22 exons across roughly 110 kilobases of DNA.⁠19 Changes in this gene account for about 40% of inherited breast cancers and over 80% of inherited breast and ovarian cancers combined.⁠19
