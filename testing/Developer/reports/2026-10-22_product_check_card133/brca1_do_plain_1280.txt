The protein it makes joins with other tumor suppressors, DNA damage sensors, and signal transducers to form a large multi-subunit protein complex known as the BRCA1-associated genome surveillance complex (BASC).⁠19 I found 1 gene related to BRCA1.⁠1

The BRCA1 gene contains 22 exons spanning about 110 kb of DNA.⁠19 This protein plays a role in transcription, DNA repair of double-stranded breaks, and recombination.⁠19 When mutations occur in this gene, they are responsible for approximately 40% of inherited breast cancers and more than 80% of inherited breast and ovarian cancers.⁠19
