BRCA1 is a 190 kD nuclear phosphoprotein that maintains genomic stability and acts as a tumor suppressor, functioning in transcription, double-strand break DNA repair, and recombination.⁠19 Found 1 gene record for BRCA1: BRCA1 DNA repair associated.⁠1

The BRCA1 gene contains 22 exons spanning about 110 kb of DNA and encodes a protein that associates with RNA polymerase II and interacts with histone deacetylase complexes through its C-terminal domain.⁠19 Alternative splicing modulates subcellular localization and physiological function, and many alternatively spliced transcript variants, some disease-associated, have been described.⁠19
