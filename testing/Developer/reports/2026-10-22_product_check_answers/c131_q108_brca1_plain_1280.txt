I found 5 published papers related to BRCA1.⁠1–5

In basic terms, BRCA1 is a gene that produces a large protein acting as a tumor suppressor and helping keep the genome stable.⁠19 The BRCA1 protein joins with other tumor-suppressing proteins and DNA-damage sensors to form a large complex, and it is involved in transcription, DNA break repair, and recombination.⁠19 Changes in this gene account for roughly 40% of inherited breast cancers and over 80% of inherited breast and ovarian cancers combined.⁠19
