Found 5 papers for BRCA1.⁠1–5

DNA REPAIR AND REPLICATION

BRCA1 promotes DNA double-strand break repair by homologous recombination and protects replication forks from attrition, partnering with BARD1 and other cofactors to mediate DNA resection and RAD51 recruitment.⁠19

ENDOCRINE AND METABOLIC REGULATION

Correlations between metabolic disorders including diabetes and the metabolic syndrome and BRCA1 mutations have also been discussed.⁠20

STROMAL NICHE AND TUMOR INITIATION

In BRCA1+/mut carriers, the premalignant stromal niche promotes epithelial proliferation and mutant BRCA1-driven tumorigenesis in trans, with pre-cancer-associated fibroblasts producing protumorigenic factors including MMP3.⁠21
