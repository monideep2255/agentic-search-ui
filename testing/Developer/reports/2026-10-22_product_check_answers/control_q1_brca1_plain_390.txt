BRCA1 is associated with four diseases: Familial cancer of breast, Familial breast-ovarian cancer susceptibility 1, Pancreatic cancer susceptibility 4, and Fanconi anemia complementation group S.⁠1–4 I found 4 conditions related to BRCA1.⁠1–4

BRCA1 is a gene that helps keep your DNA stable and stops tumors from forming.⁠23 When this gene is changed, it can lead to inherited forms of breast and ovarian cancer.⁠23 The gene also helps repair damaged DNA.⁠23
