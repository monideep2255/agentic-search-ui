Needs fixing

Most serious first. The two major ones are about the answers themselves, which is the product's reason to exist.

Major

Follow-up questions do not work

Asking a follow-up after an answer gets refused. The product's own suggested chip, "What variants cause it?", was refused as off-topic the first time and as "no grounded evidence" the second.

The second screenshot shows why: the NCBI fallback link searches for the words What variants cause it? literally. "It" was never connected to BRCA1.

Working

Checked in the browser and confirmed from the screenshots.

  • WorksDisclaimer. Continue stays disabled until the box is ticked, Escape does not close it, and it returns after Log out.
  • WorksProgress screen. Five steps and Stop appear while a search runs.
  • WorksWhen an answer comes back. Diseases named in words, citation chips on each claim, and all 4 links point to ncbi.nlm.nih.gov.
  • WorksOff-topic refusal. "What is the capital of France?" is refused instantly and never mentions Paris.
  • WorksNew search. Clears the conversation and returns to the home page.
  • WorksMistyped address. /nonsense shows the Search page, not a blank one.
  • WorksIntegrations, About, Docs. Each opens, the copy button copies its snippet, and the back button works.
  • WorksAccount menu. Email, "Signed in · no search limit in effect yet", API key and integrations, Documentation, and a red Log out.
  • WorksHistory panel. Hides and comes back.
  • WorksLog out. Email and the whole conversation disappear from the screen.
  • WorksDuplicate sign-up. "Could not create that account. That email may already be registered."
  • WorksAnswer depth. When Clinical brief answers, it uses plain wording with no advice. Deep technical answered with 5 sources.

Is your checklist comprehensive?

Yes, for everything a person can do by hand. I checked it against all 50 developer workflows and added three that were missing: a question with no data, too many guest attempts, and feedback when the connection drops. That makes 21 tests.

Three workflows cannot be triggered by hand, and they stay on the developer side:

  • a citation pointing outside NCBI
  • the shared daily guest limit
  • how the answer text arrives on screen

How this was tested
