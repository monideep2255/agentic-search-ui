The typical symptoms of GERD are heartburn and regurgitation of gastric contents into the oropharynx.⁠13 Found 5 pubmed records, 1 medgen record, 1 literature entity record and 5 clinical trial records for GERD.⁠1–12

TYPICAL SYMPTOMS AND RISK FACTORS

GERD affects quality of life and may cause erosive esophagitis, esophageal strictures, and Barrett esophagus, a precursor to esophageal adenocarcinoma.⁠13

Factors playing a role in GERD pathogenesis include abnormal transient relaxations of the lower esophageal sphincter, defects in esophageal mucosal defense, impaired esophageal and gastric motility and clearance, and anatomical defects such as hiatal hernia.⁠14 The clinical manifestations of GERD in young children are varied and nonspecific, prompting the necessity for careful diagnostic evaluation.⁠14

GERD is a clinical diagnosis most effectively treated with proton-pump inhibitors.⁠13 Long-term PPI use is associated with bone fractures, chronic renal disease, acute renal disease, community-acquired pneumonia, and Clostridium difficile intestinal infection.⁠13 Management should be targeted to the underlying aetiopathogenesis and to limit complications of GERD.⁠14
