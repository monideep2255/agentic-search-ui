What are the typical symptoms and risk factors of GERD?

The typical symptoms of gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD) are heartburn and regurgitation of gastric contents into the oropharynx, and the condition arises from reflux of stomach contents into the esophagus or oral cavity causing symptoms or complications.⁠13 Found 5 pubmed records, 1 medgen record, 1 literature entity record and 5 clinical trial records for GERD.⁠1–12

RISK FACTORS AND PATHOGENESIS

From the paper "Gastroesophageal reflux disease in children: What's new right now?": Pathogenic factors include abnormal transient relaxations of the lower esophageal sphincter, defects in esophageal mucosal defense, impaired esophageal and gastric motility and clearance, and anatomical defects of the lower esophageal reflux barrier such as hiatal hernia.⁠14

GERD may also lead to erosive esophagitis, esophageal strictures, and Barrett esophagus, a precursor to esophageal adenocarcinoma.⁠13
