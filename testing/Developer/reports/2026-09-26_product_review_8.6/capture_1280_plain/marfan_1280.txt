Marfan syndrome is a connective tissue disease that can affect many parts of the body.⁠1

MedGen lists these clinical features: Aortic regurgitation, Arachnodactyly, Astigmatism, Ectopia lentis, Esotropia, Exotropia, Pes planus, Glaucoma, Congestive heart failure, Hypertropia and Micrognathia.⁠2–12
