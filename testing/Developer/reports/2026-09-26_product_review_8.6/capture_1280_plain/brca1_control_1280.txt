I found 1 gene related to BRCA1.⁠2

This protein is involved in transcribing genes, fixing DNA where both strands have broken, and recombining DNA.⁠1

Changes in this gene account for roughly 40% of inherited breast cancers and over 80% of inherited breast and ovarian cancers.⁠1
