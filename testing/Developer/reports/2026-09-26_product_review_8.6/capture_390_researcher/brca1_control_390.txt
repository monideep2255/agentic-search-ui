Found 1 gene record for BRCA1: BRCA1 DNA repair associated.⁠4

DNA REPAIR FUNCTION

The BRCA1-BARD1 complex promotes RAD51-mediated homologous DNA pairing and functions in DNA end resection and DNA protection.⁠1, 2 The gene product assembles into the BRCA1-associated genome surveillance complex alongside other tumor suppressors and DNA damage sensors.⁠3
