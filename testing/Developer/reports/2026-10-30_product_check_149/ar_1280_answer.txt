Upon binding its hormone ligand, the receptor dissociates from accessory proteins, translocates into the nucleus, dimerizes, and stimulates transcription of androgen-responsive genes.⁠19 Found 1 gene record for AR: androgen receptor.⁠1

GENE STRUCTURE AND FUNCTION

The androgen receptor gene is more than 90 kb long and codes for a protein with three major functional domains: the N-terminal domain, DNA-binding domain, and androgen-binding domain.⁠19 The gene contains polymorphic trinucleotide repeat segments encoding polyglutamine and polyglycine tracts in the N-terminal transactivation domain.⁠19

DISEASE ASSOCIATIONS

Expansion of the polyglutamine tract from the normal 9–34 repeats to the pathogenic 38–62 repeats causes spinal bulbar muscular atrophy, also known as Kennedy's disease.⁠19 Mutations in this gene are also associated with complete androgen insensitivity.⁠19
