GCK is associated with four diseases: Type 2 diabetes mellitus, Maturity-onset diabetes of the young type 2, Hyperinsulinemic hypoglycemia, familial, 3, and Permanent neonatal diabetes mellitus 1.⁠1–4 Found 4 disease records for GCK: Type 2 diabetes mellitus, Maturity-onset diabetes of the young type 2, Hyperinsulinemic hypoglycemia, familial, 3 and Permanent neonatal diabetes mellitus 1.⁠1–4

Tissue-specific isoforms in the pancreas mediate glucose-stimulated insulin secretion, while hepatic isoforms drive glucose uptake and glycogen conversion.⁠23 Mutations altering enzyme activity have been linked to multiple diabetes types and hyperinsulinemic hypoglycemia.⁠23
