## Root causes shared by several cards

1. The guard model call has a slow-burst tail, and the guardrail's two-attempt split and fail-open and fail-closed handling turn it into failed or unsafe searches. Cards: 72, 84, 50 (the 10 to 15 s the guard step can cost), and the fast-fatal sightings in the 94 and 91 diagnoses. One fix closes them together: a single guardrail design, written with the owner first, with these parts in one order: (a) log each guard call's elapsed time and serving upstream (no behaviour change, no decision), (b) one rule that a refusal beats an admit on every guard call (84's finding), (c) a hedged or re-timed second request for slow replies, and a defined path for unusable replies (not covered by 72's hedge as written), (d) copy that tells the person the guard step, not their question, failed. Cards 84 and 72 touch the same functions and must be built in sequence in one design, not two.

2. The saved copy of an answer is a different record from the live answer. Cards: 54 (cap of 50 citations in four places), 67 (outage wording baked in without a date), 71 (note, Sources, tag, pagination, duplicate listing), 59 (history and session memory record what the screen discarded). One fix: decide what the saved record is (the same rendered answer plus a stored date and a full 100 citations) and build the capture and the reopen view against one definition. Build 54 and 67 first (they complete the record), then 71 (makes the screens match), then 59 (makes Stop consistent with the record).

3. Time is bounded by waiting, not by work. Cards 14 and 72: a timeout cancels the wait but sometimes not the thread work (14) and the guard step's attempt budget cuts good work (72). Related to 50 for the tail. The one fix is outside any single card: a measurement of where the 20 s goes per step (the 8.7 trace work) so the budgets sum to the target. Not a code fix by itself.

4. State kept only in process memory. Card 36 (`_OFFERED`) and, in family, 59 (session memory written before a Stop). One fix: a clicked chip carries its own window in its text so nothing needs restoring.

## Suggested order

1. Guard-call logging for card 72 (no decision needed, no behaviour change): the next burst gets tied to an upstream host, and every later guard decision is made on data.
2. Owner decision on the guardrail design for 84 and 72 together: both are parked on the same decision, and today develop still fails about 1 search in 20 to 40 in a burst.
3. Card 54 diagnosis (the column bound) then raise the 50 caps to 100: a long answer reopened with dead citations is a wrong answer in the saved view, and the work is small once the column is known.
4. Card 67 (dated or reworded note) with card 36's honest message: both are small wording changes with no new state.
5. Card 71 after the owner picks which note is right: needs 54 and 67 landed so the saved copy is complete.
6. Card 59: after 50's screen-lag fix, because that fix shrinks the window and may let the answer simply stand.
7. Card 50 as phase 8.7, with a trace of per-step time first.
8. Card 14: re-measure; close if no run over 60 s shows in two golden runs.
