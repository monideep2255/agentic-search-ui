BRCA1 is associated with familial cancer of breast, familial breast-ovarian cancer susceptibility 1, pancreatic cancer susceptibility 4, and Fanconi anemia complementation group S.⁠1–4 The BRCA1 gene encodes a 190 kD nuclear phosphoprotein that plays a role in maintaining genomic stability and acts as a tumor suppressor, with mutations responsible for approximately 40% of inherited breast cancers and more than 80% of inherited breast and ovarian cancers.⁠5
