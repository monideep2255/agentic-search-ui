I found 4 conditions related to BRCA1.⁠1–4

The diseases associated with BRCA1 are familial cancer of breast, familial breast-ovarian cancer susceptibility 1, pancreatic cancer susceptibility 4, and Fanconi anemia complementation group S.⁠1–4 BRCA1 is a gene that makes a protein helping keep a cell's genetic material stable and acting as a safeguard against tumors.⁠5 Changes in this gene account for roughly 40% of inherited breast cancers and over 80% of inherited breast and ovarian cancers.⁠5
