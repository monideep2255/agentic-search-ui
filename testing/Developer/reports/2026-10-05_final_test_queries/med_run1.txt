Familial Mediterranean fever is a condition caused by mutations in the MEFV gene that mainly affects people of Mediterranean descent, including Turkish, Jewish, Arabic, and Armenian populations.⁠1

The study identified harmful variants in 218 distinct genes across 176 families, documenting the wide genetic diversity underlying these disorders.⁠2
