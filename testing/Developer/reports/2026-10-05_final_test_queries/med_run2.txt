I found 5 published papers on this topic.⁠4–8

Different gene mutations cause different levels of enzyme deficiency, and acute hemolysis can be triggered by infection, oxidative drugs, or fava beans.⁠1

Another set of studies focuses on Familial Mediterranean fever, a condition caused by mutations in the MEFV gene that mainly affects people of Mediterranean descent, with higher incidence in Turkish, Jewish, Arabic, and Armenian populations.⁠2

A study of hemoglobin variants found that ancestry and geography play a significant role in the incidence of these variants, with African, Asian, and Mediterranean populations among the most affected.⁠3
