NOTES
Pathogen Detection lists 141,055 Escherichia coli isolates with these genes; the first 20 in the snapshot are shown.
Searching Escherichia coli isolates in Pathogen Detection for AMR genotypes starting blaCTX-M; blaTEM and blaSHV alleles were not searched: most are not ESBLs and the name alone cannot tell an ESBL allele from a narrow-spectrum one, so listing them would show isolates that do not carry an ESBL.
