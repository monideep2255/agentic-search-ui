MALE CARRIERS AND CLINICAL ACTIONABILITY

Male carriers face increased risk for prostate, pancreatic, and breast cancers, and PARP inhibitors have received FDA approval for BRCA1/2-associated cancers at these sites.⁠20

The BRCA1 gene encodes a 190 kD nuclear phosphoprotein that maintains genomic stability and acts as a tumor suppressor, with mutations responsible for approximately 40% of inherited breast cancers and more than 80% of inherited breast and ovarian cancers.⁠21
