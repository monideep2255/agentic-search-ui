Which diseases are associated with BRCA1?
New search
Answered6.2s · 11 sources from 2 layers

NCBI records link BRCA1 to four diseases: familial cancer of breast, familial breast-ovarian cancer susceptibility 1, pancreatic cancer susceptibility 4 and Fanconi anemia complementation group S.1–4

Each disease below comes from its own MedGen record. Five clinical trials that name BRCA1 are listed after them.7–11
