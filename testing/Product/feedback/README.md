# Feedback

Drop a screenshot or a `.md` note in `inbox/`. Name it whatever you like, then say "check the inbox".

## What happens next

I read the inbox and reply with a short list: what each item is, whether it is already known, and a one-line suggested fix. Then we decide together what to act on. Nothing gets fixed, moved or built until you say so.

## What is committed

Screenshots stay on your machine, gitignored. Any `.md` notes are committed. If a screenshot is worth keeping, say so and it gets committed deliberately.
